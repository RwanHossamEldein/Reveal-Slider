## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `reveal_slider`.
* Supports continuous multi-layer transitions with custom widgets.
* Supports both vertical and horizontal reveal directions.
* Built-in futuristic glowing scanner line (`RevealGlowBar`) with full custom divider support (`customDivider`).
* Smooth touch, hover, and drag interactions across Mobile, Web, and Desktop.
* Layer transition callbacks (`onLayerChanged`).
